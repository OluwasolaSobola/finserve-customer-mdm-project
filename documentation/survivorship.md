# Survivorship Decision Example

| Attribute | CRM | Core Banking | Mobile Banking | Golden value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full Name | John Smith | John Andrew Smith | J. Smith | John Andrew Smith | Verified, most complete identity value |
| DOB | 1985-05-12 | 1985-05-12 | 1985-05-12 | 1985-05-12 | Sources agree; trusted identity source |
| Email | john@email.com | john@email.com | JOHN@EMAIL.COM | john@email.com | Equivalent after normalisation |
| Phone | 07123456789 | 07123456789 | 07123-456789 | 07123456789 | Equivalent after normalisation |
| Address | 25 Queen Road, Swansea | 10 King Street, Swansea | — | 25 Queen Road, Swansea | Most recent verified authenticated update |

The decision is made **attribute by attribute**. One source does not automatically win every field.
